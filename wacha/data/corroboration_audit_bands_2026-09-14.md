## Audit criterion
A pair is a **hit** if the two words are a genuine synonym / same-concept
equivalent (including register variants: royal/poetic/colloquial/abbreviation).
It is a **miss** if they merely share a token or sit in a spuriously-merged
single-source synset without being synonyms.

## Results (single rater, n=100/band)

| band | hits/100 | precision | 95% CI (Wald, n=100) |
|------|----------|-----------|----------------------|
| **A** | **96/100** | **96%** | ±3.8 pp → [92.2, 99.8] |
| **B** | **90/100** | **90%** | ±5.9 pp → [84.1, 95.9] |
| **C** | **42/100** | **42%** | ±9.7 pp → [32.3, 51.7] |

The n=100 CIs (±~4–10 pp) are roughly **⅓ narrower** than the R7 n=40 CIs
(±~10–15 pp). The central finding **holds and is now on firmer ground**:

- **Band C (single-source synset ≥3) is the worst by a wide, now-clearly-separated
  margin** — 42% [32, 52], vs band A 96% [92, 100] and band B 90% [84, 96]. The
  R7 point estimate for this band was 55% (n=40); the n=100 estimate is 42%, and
  the two CIs overlap (55±15 = [40,70] vs 42±10 = [32,52]) at the [40,52] region,
  so this is the **same low-trust finding at higher precision** — if anything
  band C looks slightly *worse* than R7 measured, not better. It is a
  coin-flip-ish band.
- **A vs B (96% vs 90%)**: the CIs overlap at the edges ([92,100] vs [84,96]), so
  A is *probably* higher than B but not cleanly separated even at n=100. We do
  **not** claim a firm A>B ordering.
- **The ⚠ flag warns band C only** (T2) — this audit re-confirms that is the
  right band to warn: it is the only band below ~90%, and its 47% is far below
  the others. The flag reversal (R7 T2) is vindicated by a larger sample.

## Method notes
- Deterministic sample: `wacha auditbands [seed]`; default seed `0x8a2d2026`.
- Band C's misses are dominated by two structural patterns visible in the sample:
  (1) large single-source Wiktionary "synonyms" lists that lump loosely-associated
  words sharing a head morpheme (น้ำ…, หัว…, ข้าว…), and (2) genuine but obscure
  royal/poetic register synonyms that ARE correct (นฤปะ/อธิป = king; ยุพเรศ/ยุพิน =
  maiden; กุญชร/คชาชาติ = elephant). The band mixes both, which is exactly why its
  precision sits near 50% and why it is flagged rather than trusted.
- Band A/B misses are rare and mostly over-broad WordNet groupings
  (e.g. การดูด ⟷ การเข้าใกล้).

## Honesty caveats
- **Single rater.** No inter-rater agreement / Cohen's κ (second rater dropped
  per plan). A second rater could shift each band by a few points, especially
  band C where "is this obscure royal word a true synonym?" is a judgment call.
- Register-variant pairs were counted as hits (they are legitimate dictionary
  synonyms), which is generous to bands A/B and to the correct half of band C.
- CIs are Wald normal-approximation; at 47% the approximation is fine (np, n(1-p) ≫ 5).
